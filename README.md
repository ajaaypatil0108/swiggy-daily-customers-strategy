# Making Swiggy a Daily Habit — Product Strategy

A product strategy to grow Swiggy's daily customers by winning the weekday lunch: three strategies, checked against what Swiggy had already tried, with a bear/base/bull estimate of the extra orders they could add, built on Swiggy's FY24 figures.

**[View the case study site](https://ajaaypatil0108.github.io/swiggy-daily-customers-strategy/)** · **[Download the PDF](Swiggy_Daily_Customers_Strategy.pdf)**

*Ajay Patil · Personal project · Feb 2024 · Revised Oct 2026*

## What's inside

| Section | What it covers |

## Key numbers

| Metric | Bear | Base | Bull |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extra orders on an average day | +26,830 | +96,625 | +227,504 |
| Increase vs FY24 baseline (1,578,415 a day) | +1.7% | +6.1% | +14.4% |

**Main finding:** most users order less than once a week, and the meal they need every day, weekday lunch, is the one Swiggy barely serves. Cheap home-style lunches can add daily orders, but only if deliveries are batched, and any reward for ordering more has to cost less than the roughly ₹24 each extra order contributes.

## What changed from the Feb 2024 draft

- **Competitors corrected:** removed Uber Eats, which sold its India business to Zomato in January 2020; noted that Dunzo shut down in January 2025; added Zomato Everyday and local tiffin services.
- **Facts corrected:** "Swiggy Genie" is a courier service, not the name for delivery partners. Removed the unverified commission rate and delivery-time claims.
- **Strategies reframed:** focused on the three strategies from the draft's final section, and checked each against what Swiggy had already tried. Swiggy Daily (2019) had tested home-style meals; Swiggy later relaunched it in May 2024.
- **Unsupported percentages replaced:** the draft's 5–20% per-strategy claims (30–55% combined) are replaced with an estimate built on Swiggy's FY24 figures and stated assumptions.
- **Added:** unit economics, a pilot plan with control groups, KPIs, risks and sources.
- Removed third-party logos and stock photos, and fixed typos.
